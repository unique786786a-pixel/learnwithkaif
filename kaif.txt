DESIGN SYSTEM
- Dark editorial base with light sections for contrast
  - Background #0B0B0D, surface #141417, text #F5F5F0, muted #A1A1AA
  - Light section bg #F5F5F0 with ink text #0B0B0D
  - Accent: brand gold, sampled from the logo (about #F2B544 to #E0A12E gradient, used flat or as a very subtle gradient). Use it sparingly for CTAs, key numbers, underlines, hover states and small details.
- Typography: Manrope or Inter. Oversized, tight-leading bold headlines (clamp-based fluid sizing), generous whitespace, strong hierarchy.
- Style: minimal, premium, editorial grids, subtle glassmorphism only on the navbar and floating elements, restrained rounded corners, no stock photos, no heavy gradients.
- Motion (subtle, fast): hero text fade-up on load, section reveal on scroll, card lift on hover, count-up numbers, marquee, animated process line, scroll progress bar. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
